## 1. 🍕 Recipe: Authentic Domino's-Style Cheese Burst Pizza

```markdown
# 🍕 Authentic Domino's-Style Cheese Burst Pizza Recipe

Here is a complete, mouth-watering recipe for an **authentic Cheese Burst Pizza** with a molten, liquid cheese center inside the crust, just like Domino's!

**Yield:** 1 10-inch pizza | **Prep time:** 25 mins | **Baking time:** 12–15 mins at 220°C (425°F)

---

### 🛒 Ingredients

#### A. Pizza Dough
- **All-purpose flour / Bread flour:** 2 cups (250g)
- **Instant active yeast:** 1 tsp
- **Warm water:** ¾ cup (~180ml, around 40°C / 105°F)
- **Sugar:** 1 tsp (to activate yeast)
- **Salt:** ½ tsp
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 2 tbsp
- **Fine semolina / cornmeal:** 2 tbsp (for dusting & crispy undercrust)

#### B. The Secret Cheese Burst Center (Molten Core)
- **Processed liquid cheese spread (Cheez Whiz, Amul, or Britannia):** ½ cup
- **Heavy cream or warm milk:** 2 tbsp (prevents cheese from solidifying while cooling)
- **Shredded Mozzarella:** ¼ cup

#### C. Pizza Sauce & Toppings
- **Classic marinara or spiced pizza sauce:** ⅓ cup
- **Shredded low-moisture Mozzarella:** 1.5 cups
- **Vegetable toppings:**
  - Diced capsicum / green bell pepper (¼ cup)
  - Sliced red onions (¼ cup)
  - Sweet corn kernels (steamed, 2 tbsp)
  - Pickled jalapeño slices (1 tbsp)
- **Herb seasoning:** 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil/thyme, ½ tsp red chili flakes
- **Garlic butter crust wash:** 2 tbsp melted salted butter + 1 grated garlic clove

---

### 👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

#### Step 1: Dough Preparation & First Rise
1. **Activate Yeast:** In a mixing bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and instant yeast. Let rest for 5–7 minutes until frothy.
2. **Knead:** Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Knead vigorously by hand for 8–10 minutes (or 5 minutes in a stand mixer) until smooth, silky, and elastic.
3. **First Proof:** Grease a bowl with olive oil, place the dough ball inside, cover with a damp cloth or cling wrap, and let double in size in a warm spot (approx. 60–75 minutes).

#### Step 2: The Double-Crust Secret (Cheese Burst Assembly)
1. **Divide the Dough:** Deflate the proofed dough gently. Divide into **two unequal balls**:
   - **Smaller ball (⅓ of dough):** For the thin inner liquid cheese barrier.
   - **Larger ball (⅔ of dough):** For the bottom and outer crust.
2. **Roll & Par-bake the Inner Pocket:**
   - Roll the **smaller dough ball** into a thin 7-inch round disc.
   - Dock all over with a fork to prevent bubbling.
   - Cook on a dry non-stick tawa/skillet over medium heat for **45 seconds per side** until semi-cooked and pale (do not brown). Transfer to a plate to cool.
3. **Roll the Base Crust:**
   - Dust your pizza tray or parchment paper with fine cornmeal.
   - Roll the **larger dough ball** out to a 10-inch circle.
4. **Fill the Molten Core:**
   - Whisk the cheese spread and heavy cream together until silky.
   - Spread the cheese mixture generously over the 10-inch base, leaving a 1.2-inch outer border clean.
5. **Seal the Burst:**
   - Place the cooled 7-inch par-baked disc squarely on top of the liquid cheese layer.
   - Lift the 1.2-inch outer border of the bottom crust, fold it inward over the edge of the top disc, and crimp firmly with moist fingertips or a fork to form a leak-proof double crust seal.

#### Step 3: Top and Bake to Perfection
1. **Sauce:** Spread ⅓ cup of pizza sauce across the top crust.
2. **Layer Cheese & Veggies:** Scatter half of the shredded mozzarella, distribute the sweet corn, onions, bell peppers, and jalapeños, then cover with remaining mozzarella.
3. **Garlic Butter Glaze:** Brush the crimped outer crust rim with garlic butter and sprinkle with Italian herbs.
4. **Bake:** Slide into an oven preheated to its maximum setting (**220°C–240°C / 430°F–465°F**). Bake on the lower-middle rack for **12–15 minutes** until the crust is golden-brown and the cheese is bubbling with toasted spots.
5. **Serve:** Let rest for 2 minutes, slice into wedges, and watch the molten cheese river burst out!

---

### 💡 Chef's Pro Tips
- **Why par-cook the inner layer?** Par-baking the small dough disc for 45 seconds prevents the dough from absorbing moisture from the cheese spread, guaranteeing zero sogginess.
- **Ultra-Gooey Melt:** Mixing processed cheese spread with heavy cream keeps the interior liquid even 15 minutes after baking.
```
