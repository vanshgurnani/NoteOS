# 🍕 Authentic Domino's-Style Cheese Burst Pizza Recipe

> **Category:** Culinary / Recipes  
> **Yield:** 1 10-inch pizza  
> **Prep Time:** 25 mins  
> **Rise Time:** 60 mins  
> **Bake Time:** 12–15 mins at 220°C–240°C (425°F–465°F)  

Here is the ultimate recipe for an authentic, restaurant-grade **Cheese Burst Pizza** with a molten, liquid cheese center inside the crust, exactly like Domino's!

---

## 🛒 Ingredients

### A. Pizza Dough
- **All-purpose flour or bread flour:** 2 cups (250g)
- **Instant active dry yeast:** 1 tsp
- **Warm water:** ¾ cup (~180ml, ~40°C / 105°F)
- **Granulated sugar:** 1 tsp
- **Salt:** ½ tsp
- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 2 tbsp
- **Fine cornmeal / semolina:** 2 tbsp (for dusting & crispy crust base)

### B. The Secret Cheese Burst Center (Liquid Core)
- **Processed liquid cheese spread (Cheez Whiz, Amul, or Britannia):** ½ cup
- **Heavy cream or warm whole milk:** 2 tbsp *(secret to keeping it molten after cooling)*
- **Shredded Mozzarella:** ¼ cup

### C. Sauce & Toppings
- **Classic marinara or spiced pizza sauce:** ⅓ cup
- **Low-moisture whole milk Mozzarella (shredded):** 1.5 cups
- **Vegetable toppings:**
  - Sweet corn kernels (steamed): 2 tbsp
  - Sliced red onions: ¼ cup
  - Diced capsicum / green bell pepper: ¼ cup
  - Pickled jalapeño slices: 1 tbsp
- **Italian seasoning:** 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp dried basil, ½ tsp chili flakes
- **Garlic butter crust glaze:** 2 tbsp melted salted butter + 1 grated garlic clove

---

## 👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Yeast Dough
1. In a small bowl, whisk warm water, sugar, and yeast. Allow to stand for 5–7 minutes until bubbly and frothy.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour and salt. Create a well in the center and pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil.
3. Knead vigorously for 8–10 minutes until you achieve a soft, elastic, supple dough that springs back when pressed.
4. Shape into a ball, grease lightly with olive oil, place in a covered bowl, and let proof in a warm spot for 60 minutes until doubled in size.

### Step 2: Assemble the Double-Crust Cheese Burst
1. **Divide the Dough:** Gently deflate the proofed dough. Divide it into **2 unequal balls**:
   - **⅓ portion (Smaller ball):** For the thin inner liquid cheese barrier.
   - **⅔ portion (Larger ball):** For the bottom base and folded outer crust.
2. **Roll & Par-cook the Inner Pocket:**
   - Roll the smaller portion into a very thin 7-inch circle.
   - Prick all over with a fork so steam escapes and it does not puff up.
   - Heat a dry flat tawa or skillet over medium heat. Cook the thin disc for **45 seconds per side** until semi-firm and dry (do not brown). Set aside to cool.
3. **Roll the Base Crust:**
   - Dust your pizza stone, pan, or parchment paper generously with fine cornmeal.
   - Roll the larger dough portion into a 10-inch circle.
4. **Spread the Liquid Cheese:**
   - Whisk the cheese spread with the heavy cream until smooth and glossy.
   - Spread this mixture evenly over the 10-inch base, leaving a **1.2-inch border** around the edge.
5. **Seal the Cheese Burst:**
   - Place the cooled 7-inch par-cooked disc squarely over the liquid cheese layer.
   - Lift the outer 1.2-inch border of the base dough up and fold it over the edges of the 7-inch top disc.
   - Press and pleat firmly with your fingers or crimp with a fork. Ensure an airtight seal so no cheese leaks during baking.

### Step 3: Top and Bake
1. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the top 7-inch crust.
2. Scatter half of the shredded mozzarella across the sauce.
3. Arrange your vegetables (sweet corn, onions, capsicum, jalapeños).
4. Blanket generously with the remaining mozzarella cheese.
5. Brush the crimped outer crust rim with the garlic butter and sprinkle Italian herbs.
6. Slide into an oven preheated to its highest setting (**220°C–240°C / 430°F–465°F**).
7. Bake for **12–15 minutes** until the crust is deep golden, the edges are blistered, and the cheese is bubbling with toasted spots.
8. Let rest for 2 minutes, slice into wedges, and serve hot!

---

## 💡 Chef's Pro Tips
- **No Soggy Crust:** Par-baking the small dough disc for 45 seconds creates a barrier that prevents the sauce from mixing with the liquid cheese and keeps the crust crispy.
- **Continuous Liquid Burst:** Always blend processed cheese spread with heavy cream or milk; pure mozzarella solidifies quickly once taken out of the oven, whereas processed cheese spread remains molten.
- **Crispy Bottom:** Using cornmeal / semolina on the bottom of the crust gives that signature Domino's crunch.
